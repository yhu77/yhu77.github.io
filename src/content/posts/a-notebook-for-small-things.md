---
title: "A notebook for small things"
description: "Why ordinary observations often become the most durable raw material for meaningful work."
publishedAt: 2026-07-29
category: Attention
---

Most ideas do not arrive as ideas. They arrive as a sentence overheard, a question that lingers, or a tiny moment that feels more interesting than it should.

A notebook gives those fragments somewhere to wait. The practice is not about recording everything. It is about signaling to yourself that small things are worth noticing.

Over time, patterns emerge. Repeated questions become essays. Brief observations become decisions. Attention compounds quietly.
