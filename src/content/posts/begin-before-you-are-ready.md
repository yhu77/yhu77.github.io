---
title: "Begin before you’re ready"
description: "A practical note on lowering the stakes and letting the first version be small."
publishedAt: 2026-08-12
category: Creative practice
---

Readiness is often perfectionism wearing sensible clothes. It tells us to do more research, find better tools, or wait for a quieter season.

But beginnings are supposed to be incomplete. Their job is not to prove that the idea will work. Their job is to reveal what the next step actually is.

Choose a version small enough to finish this week. Give it a name. Put it somewhere real. Then let the response—especially your own response—teach you what it wants to become.
