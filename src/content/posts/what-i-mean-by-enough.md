---
title: "What I mean by enough"
description: "Thinking about ambition without allowing it to turn every part of life into a project."
publishedAt: 2026-07-08
category: Life
---

“Enough” can sound like settling, but it can also be a boundary drawn with care. It gives ambition a shape instead of allowing it to expand forever.

The difficult part is that no metric can define enough for us. We have to decide what a good day feels like, what kind of work deserves our energy, and what we refuse to optimize.

Enough is not the end of growth. It is the point at which growth becomes a choice again.
